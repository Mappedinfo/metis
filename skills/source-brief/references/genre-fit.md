# Genre and material

Use [shared writing judgment](writing-foundations.en.md) for situation,
evidence, history and comparison decisions. These forms only supplement genre
selection. Available material can support short or long writing as requested;
length does not authorize new facts.

| Form | Organization | Check |
|---|---|---|
| Decision memo | Choice, comparable evidence, tradeoffs, recommendation and unknowns | Separate proposed from accepted decisions; invent no costs or support |
| Progress update | Changed state, evidence, consequence and next action | Separate completed, attempted, blocked and unverified work; a commit is not deployment |
| Explanation/tutorial | Reader question, necessary terms, examples/steps and recovery | Preserve exact commands/fields and state examples' conditions |
| Source-grounded narrative | Established sequence, actions, consequences and narrator knowledge | Add no invented scene, dialogue, thoughts or user experience; attribute judgments |
| Selective technical history | Contemporary problems, conditions, methods, evidence and later continuities or changes | State period and scope; distinguish parallel traditions; order alone proves neither influence nor replacement |

If factual material is insufficient, identify the smallest gap and deliver the
supported portion only where useful. Repetition cannot make a requested length
supportable; keep an unmet length requirement blocked. Label authorized teaching
examples as hypothetical/illustrative, not observations. Fiction requires a
separate suitable capability; a source map is not a fiction-writing method.

Genre changes organization, not the scenario's responsibility for complete
delivery, and adds no review passes.

Method provenance: independently rewritten, with owner authorization, from
`human-writing` and `writing-quality` genre selection, factual-material boundaries
and semantic checks. Personal voice defaults, fixed material-count/length gates,
private configuration, historical cases and dedicated dependencies are excluded.

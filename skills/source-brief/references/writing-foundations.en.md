# Shared writing judgment

This is the synchronized English translation of the [Chinese authored source](writing-foundations.md).
Both maintain the same ownership, scope and checks. This is an internal module
for writing scenarios, not a skill identity or another routing pass. The scenario
owner remains responsible for the complete reader-facing delivery; situation
analysis, organization, drafting and review are internal work.

## Situation and scope

Resolve the object, purpose, readers' prior knowledge, use, genre, delivery format
and available evidence from the current request. Settle known matters internally;
do not create a planning form for every task or repeatedly ask about routine
choices. Ask only when missing information materially changes the outcome or
authority. An outline, situation diagnosis, translation or local polish request
stays within that operation; it does not expand into full drafting, research or
publication.

Separate explicit requirements, claims needing verification, examples and search
leads. Technologies, products or cases casually named by the user may reveal a
confusion; they do not automatically become sections, categories or comparison
objects. For beginners especially, establish the topic's own relationships rather
than requiring the reader to decide whether supplied examples occupy the same level.

## Determine what the material supports

Distinguish facts, source interpretations, your inferences, teaching examples and
unfinished work. Consequential claims lead back to inspected source locations;
availability does not establish reading. Preserve limits on causal, performance,
generalization and current-status claims that the source does not establish.
Fluent prose cannot settle missing or conflicting evidence. Teaching examples may
simplify with stated assumptions, but do not replace observations. Reuse scenario
capabilities for retrieval, citation formats and specialist judgment rather than
duplicating their implementation here.

## Information organization

Choose a structure that answers the question rather than filling fixed headings:

- A concept explanation moves from an accessible definition and concrete example
  to mechanism, conditions and necessary alternatives.
- Instructions follow the actual task, its results and recovery from failures.
- Decision writing organizes comparable choices, evidence, tradeoffs and unknowns;
  progress writing organizes changed states and their evidence.
- History explains changes in problems, methods, evidentiary conditions and
  institutions or tools; a list of famous models is not a history.

Each section resolves a new question and supplies the next section's prerequisites.
The outline, figures, title and conclusion express the same narrative. If a draft's
organization fails, repair the affected structure and keep defenses responding to
chat corrections or abandoned drafts in working records. Retain old theories,
counterevidence, real debates and comparisons when they serve the current argument;
the user need not name them in advance.

Trace claims, support and conclusions through the draft: provide prerequisites
before the inference they enable. Transitions, causal claims and parallel points
need an actual relationship, not just a connective. A concession should identify
an evidenced condition and its effect on the conclusion; revise formulaic
concessions that do no work. Merge repeated points unless they add evidence or
advance the reasoning. Identify missing support rather than inventing a cause
to smooth a transition.

## Positive explanation and correct demonstrations

Explain the actual object's mechanism, the correct procedure and its basis
directly, using a correct demonstration the reader can follow. Skill instructions
likewise state the intended behavior; invented misreadings or incorrect procedures
are not teaching entry points.

A negative contrast requires a real, relevant and already introduced basis for
the rejected description in the current context. When another actual method is
involved, name it, the conditions under which it uses the compared approach, and
the specific question the comparison answers. Without that basis, explain how
the current object works directly.

When revising, rebuild the positive mechanism or procedure from source facts,
then check dimensions, units, terminology and necessary qualifications. Retain
responses to misconceptions the user has actually raised, as well as scientific
counterevidence, substantive limits or safety conditions that affect the
conclusion; explain their basis and consequences. Judge these by their role,
rather than deleting them mechanically because they contain negation.

## Scientific and technical history

Determine whether the history concerns theory, methods, engineering or products,
and set its period, topic boundary and narrative scale. Establish periods through
contemporary problems, material, tools, evaluation, limits and turning points in
primary or reliable historical sources. Explain the problems people faced, their
data and computational or instrumental conditions, their methods, and what later
work continued or changed. Present supported parallel traditions, continuities,
intersections, breaks and debates rather than an upgrade list. Publication order
alone establishes neither influence, motive, causality nor replacement. Distinguish
papers, systems, datasets, evaluations and deployments rather than forcing levels
into a product competition; separately assess theoretical refutation, prominence
and benchmark leadership.

State source identity and actual inspected coverage: papers, project pages,
recollections and secondary commentary support different claims. Distinguish
first proposal, preprint, formal publication and adoption-at-scale dates. Recent
results do not establish an inevitable endpoint for early research or justify
inventing undocumented contemporary motives from later success.

A selective technical history may follow one supported thread and state its scope;
it must not present a small selection as the whole field's history. Explain
influence through contemporary tasks and evidence, not merely hindsight about
being “more advanced” or “moving toward intelligence.” Check dates, priority and
inheritance claims; insufficient evidence does not support definite historical fact.

## Comparison: serve an actual judgment

State the question, common task or explicit relationship axis before selecting
objects. Architecture, training objective, data, system and application are
different levels. A cross-level explanation can show how they combine, but cannot
silently rank which is stronger. Sharing a level does not itself require comparison:
omit contrasts that do not support a reader's decision or understanding. Preserve
conditions, baseline, units, denominator and evidence limits in useful comparisons.
The outline should still explain the topic without the user's incidental names.

## Review and delivery

The scenario owner reads the complete delivery: does it answer the request, follow
a coherent line, support consequential facts, fit terms and examples to the reader,
and preserve numbers, quotations, qualifications and voice? Reuse
[prose-audit](../../prose-audit/SKILL.md) and its actual-manuscript-language guide
when needed; do not copy its word lists or create a second review procedure.
Shared modules add no review passes: use one integrated review within the current
scenario budget, at most one identified-defect revision and a check of affected
context. An enabled workflow keeps its existing revision and recovery budgets.

Internal checks do not automatically create several user documents. Reuse working
records for sources and material gaps; keep ledgers out of result-only prose.
Publication, messages and knowledge-store edits remain subject to the original
request. Genuinely independent experiments or new artifacts may have phase owners,
but organizing or reviewing the same draft does not transfer final responsibility.

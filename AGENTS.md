## Maintaining skills

Before creating or translating a skill, or editing an existing language pair,
read [the language workflow](CONTRIBUTING.md#skill-languages). `SKILL.md` is
the sole discovery entry; that role does not give English semantic priority.
`prose-audit` has separately authored Chinese and English processing guides
under one shared framework. Synchronize their shared requirements while
allowing language-specific methods and examples. Resolve a shared-requirement
conflict against the accepted task and supporting evidence, identifying any
affected unresolved items; neither English nor the newest file wins by default.
Other skills retain their declared language conventions. Check
[HANDOFF.md](HANDOFF.md) for migration and validation status.

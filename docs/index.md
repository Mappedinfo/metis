features:

    details: Who you are — personal, private, owned by you. One Markdown file describing your identity, goals, preferences and boundaries. Everything else exists to serve it.
  - title: AGENTS.md
    details: The service contract — per project, committed, generic. Holds project rules and declares that it serves the adjacent USER.md.
  - title: skills/
    details: What agents can do — shared, public, reusable. Generic capability, free of any one person's identity, so anyone can share it.
  - title: metis-os
    details: <code>npx metis-os init</code> scaffolds your USER.md and writes the AGENTS.md service clause — idempotent, zero dependencies.
  - title: skill-atlas
    details: <code>npx skill-atlas</code> scans your skill collection and graphs how skills reference, route and depend on each other.
